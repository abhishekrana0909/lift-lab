        From the lab

        Three things that actually matter

        Hit your protein first

        Build every meal around a protein: dal with paneer, eggs, chicken, soya or curd. Most Indian diets fall short here, and protein is what protects your muscle in a deficit.

        Add a rep or add a kilo

        Write down every set. Next session, beat it by one rep or a little weight. That small push, week after week, is what builds muscle.

        Steps beat extra cardio

        8,000–10,000 steps a day burns more over a week than most cardio sessions, and it doesn't make you hungry. Walk after meals and take the stairs.
